About the City

How a river-landing trading post became a city of six districts and ten public institutions.

History

Adversary City's growth tracks the institutions built to serve it, from the 1847 trading post to the shopping center that opened in 2015.

Chronology of Adversary City's founding and institutions
1847Settlers establish a trading post where the Adversary River meets the harbor.
1889Adversary City incorporates as a city.
1891City University opens on Founders Walk.
1927The city formalizes its police department, replacing a system of appointed town constables.
1938Voters approve a municipal electric utility, forming City Power Station.
1948City Bank receives its charter.
1958City Hospital opens on Meridian Parkway.
1962Adversary City Airport begins scheduled service.
1971Firestation Station 4 opens on Ladderline Road.
1998The Transit Authority replaces its original depot with the Transit Plaza terminal.
2015City Shopping Center opens on Commons Boulevard.

Geography

Adversary City covers 162 square miles on the north shore of the harbor, where the Adversary River reaches open water.

The Harbor and Riverside districts sit along the river. Founders Row and Skyport sit on higher ground to the north, away from the floodplain. Civic Center and Ledger District fill the space between them, closest to the original 1847 landing.

Government

A mayor-council government runs Adversary City, with representation for every district.

Structure

Voters elect the mayor citywide and seven council members, one from each of the six districts plus one at-large seat, all serving four-year terms. The mayor's office operates out of City Hall on Charter Plaza in Civic Center.

Meetings and recent business

The Council meets at City Hall the second Tuesday of each month, and meetings are open to the public. Recent business includes a Ward Infrastructure Task Force reviewing road and utility spending by district, and an order requiring city agencies to post response-time data every quarter.
